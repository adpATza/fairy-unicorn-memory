AIRCRAFT COLOUR SKETCHES

30 unique square PNG illustrations, supporting 30 matching pairs.
25 aeroplanes and 5 helicopters, including the approved Mustang and Spitfire.

Open preview.html to browse the collection. Use Card size to review at 100 pixels,
or click any picture to open the original. Names are outside the artwork.

In the game: Deck library -> Import an image folder -> choose the images folder.
Select all 30 pictures. Suggested domain: Aircraft; style: Illustrated; complexity: Easy.
Save the deck. The game creates two identical cards from each unique picture.

Generated with built-in image_gen, following docs/Artwork and Import Guide.html.
All generation prompts and composition edits are recorded in generation.json.
Checksums are in checksums.json. Source artwork has not been modified by this script.
