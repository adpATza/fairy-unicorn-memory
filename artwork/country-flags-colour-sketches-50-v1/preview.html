Use these cards in the memory game

Import Deck A or Deck B as a ready-made selection of 25 flags. Each folder contains only its 25 PNG files. Alternatively import the images folder and choose any 2-30 of the 50 flags. One unique picture makes one matching pair automatically.

In Deck library, choose Import an image folder, select a folder, review the pictures, name your deck and save. Suggested domain: Country flags; style: Illustrated. Difficulty depends on your selection: related tricolours and the Australian/New Zealand pair make a harder game.

Each card is an original 1254 x 1254 PNG generated individually with built-in image_gen. Country names appear here and in filenames, not as labels on the cards. Brazil retains its national motto; small heraldic details are drawn interpretations. These illustrations are for recognition and matching, not flag-manufacturing diagrams.
